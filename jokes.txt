Tera skills section aisa hai jaise teri life ke decisions — bahut kuch try kiya, kuch bhi pakka nahi kiya.

Tera experience section bilkul teri commitment jaisa hai — description lamba hai, asar chhota.

Achievements section teri baatein jaisa hai — sunne mein badi lagti hain, asar nahi karti.

Teri skill list aisi hai jaise teri weekend plans — bahut kuch likha hai, follow kuch nahi hota.

Yeh resume itna dangerous hai ki ATS isko scan karte hi crash ho jaye. Software bhi nahi seh pa raha tera confusion.

Isse achha tha tu resume banata hi nahi. Ab dekh ke lagta hai future mein bhi dark days hi aane wale hain.

Recruiter tujhe select kare isse pehle uska resignation letter ready rehta. Itna strong effect hai tere resume ka.
