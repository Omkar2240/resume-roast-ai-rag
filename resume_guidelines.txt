A strong resume should be concise and easy to scan.

Project descriptions should explain what was built, technologies used, and the candidate's contribution.

Good resume bullet points should include measurable results whenever possible.

Avoid generic phrases such as hardworking, passionate, quick learner, and team player without evidence.

Technical skills should be relevant and supported by projects or experience.

Students should showcase meaningful projects that demonstrate practical technical ability.

Resume formatting should be consistent and easy to read.

Avoid listing technologies that the candidate cannot confidently explain in an interview.
